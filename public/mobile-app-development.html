Discuss your project ↗

Start with the journey your users need.

An app begins with a clear purpose: who it helps, what they need to do, and why mobile is the right place for that task. We map the essential journeys before defining screens and development scope.

iOS and Android experiences

Define the platforms your audience uses and plan a consistent experience around them. The development approach is selected during scoping based on the app’s requirements and constraints.

Booking and service apps

Plan journeys such as discovering a service, choosing a time, and managing a booking. We identify the screens, information, and business rules needed for the agreed workflow.

Product design and development

Connect interface design with the functionality behind it. We work through screen flows, interactions, and required integrations, then build and test the agreed version of the app.

GETTING STARTED

Bring your idea.
We’ll shape the next step.

Bring a description of the problem, your intended users, target platforms, essential features, and any existing systems the app must connect to. If you have sketches or a prototype, those can help clarify the first version.

Your questions, answered.

Can we start with a smaller first version?

Yes. We can define an initial version around the core user journey, separating essential features from ideas that can be considered after launch.

Do you plan for both iOS and Android?

We discuss the platforms your customers need during discovery. The project scope defines which platforms are included and how the app will be developed for them.

What needs to be tested before launch?

The agreed user journeys, navigation, forms, and integrations need validation, including relevant device and screen-size checks. Testing is planned around the app’s features.

Can international businesses work with AVUNOR?

Yes. We work with customers worldwide, agreeing on project communication, requirements, and milestones at the start.
