Your questions, answered.

Can you work with customers worldwide?

Yes. AVUNOR offers website development to customers worldwide. Communication arrangements and delivery milestones are agreed when the project is scoped.

Can you redesign an existing website?

We can discuss a redesign around your current content, business needs, and the problems you want to solve. Share the current URL and the changes you have in mind.

Will the website work on mobile?

Responsive layouts are part of our website development approach. The pages and key interactions are checked on phone, tablet, and desktop-sized screens.

How are cost and delivery dates decided?

They depend on the required pages, design work, content, and functionality. We agree on the project scope before confirming a quotation and delivery plan.
